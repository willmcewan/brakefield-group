Questions

Before you book

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can. We run one vehicle, so dates are genuinely limited — Ole Miss home weekends and graduation go first. If it's last minute, call or text 662.715.4477 anyway and we'll tell you straight away whether the date is open.

What will you arrive in?

A black high-roof Mercedes-Benz Sprinter. It's the same vehicle every time, so you know exactly what's pulling up — and there's room for the group and the luggage together rather than split across separate cars.

Do you run to Memphis International?

Yes — Memphis International (MEM) is our most requested run. We also serve University-Oxford Airport (UOX) and Tupelo Regional (TUP). We watch your flight and adjust for delays, so a late landing doesn't leave you stranded.

How do you price a trip?

Every trip is quoted individually on distance, duration and timing, so we don't post a number that would be wrong for most people. You'll have the full rate in writing before you commit, and it doesn't change on the day.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in North Mississippi and regularly run Oxford, Tupelo, Southaven, Batesville, New Albany, Holly Springs and the surrounding communities, plus Memphis and the wider Mid-South. Going further? Ask us.

Can you handle multiple stops or wait time?

Yes. Hourly and multi-stop bookings are common for gamedays and evenings out — your driver stays with the group for the window you've booked.
